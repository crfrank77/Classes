# MAE 227 Convex Optimization for Engineers

MAE 227 focuses on convex optimization theory, convexification of non-convex problems, engineering applications, modeling and implementation in a programming language. This course covers convex sets and functions, convex optimization problems (LP, QP, SOCP, SDP), weak and strong duality, optimality conditions, and solution and shadow price interpretation. Some applications include design in mechanical engineering, optimal control problems, machine learning, energy, transportation, etc.

# Course Project

This course had groups of graduate students analyze a specific application or subfield within convex optimization through a comprehensive literature review. My group synthesized recent research on convex
optimization as a critical tool for managing Distributed Energy Resources and their connections to electrical grid systems. By examining applications in PV-BESS systems, microgrids, and distributed control architectures, our review evaluated how different mathematical formulations, such as Second Order Cone Programs (SOCP) and Linear Programming models (LP), balance computational speed with physical accuracy. Ultimately, our review identified current limitations in model assumptions and highlighted future research needs, with a focus on scalability and integration of diverse renewable energy sources to support a reliable and optimized smart grid. This folder contains the final presentation and document associated with this review.
